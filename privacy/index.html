Everdot Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Everdot is an iPhone app for tracking medicines, habits and spending. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it. The copy in the app (Settings → Privacy Policy) says the same thing.

Everdot doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer can't see anything you record. On the App Store, Everdot's privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Where your data is

Everything you record (your medicines and doses, habits, spending, and when you logged them) is stored:

  • on your iPhone, and
  • in your own private iCloud, if you use iCloud. Everdot stores it in your iCloud account's private database, which only you can read. The developer has no access to it. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.

Everdot doesn't run any servers and doesn't send your data anywhere else.

Purchases

Everdot Pro is a subscription sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment; Everdot only learns whether your subscription is active, and never sees your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Face ID

If you turn on the lock, iOS checks your face, fingerprint or passcode. Everdot only learns whether unlocking succeeded. It never receives biometric data.

Your free trial

The start date of your 14-day trial is kept in your iPhone's Keychain (and, if you use iCloud Keychain, on your other devices), so deleting and reinstalling the app doesn't restart it. Nothing else is stored there.

Exports

Export as CSV creates a spreadsheet of what you've logged. It only leaves your iPhone if you choose to share or save it, and then it goes where you send it.

Deleting your data

Delete the app from your iPhone, then remove Everdot's iCloud data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Storage → Everdot. Everdot keeps no copy anywhere else.

Children

Everdot isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, of any age.

Medical information

Everdot records what you enter. It doesn't give medical advice, check doses or warn about interactions. See Settings → Medical Disclaimer in the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date, and noted in the app's release notes.
