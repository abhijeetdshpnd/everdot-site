My data isn't syncing between devices

Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, with iCloud on and some free iCloud storage. Open Everdot on each device; changes usually arrive within a minute. Settings → iCloud Sync in Everdot shows whether sync can work.

Subscriptions

Everdot Pro is a yearly subscription through the App Store. To cancel or change it, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or tap Manage Subscription in Everdot's Settings. If a renewal fails, Pro stays on during Apple's grace period; update your payment method there.

Bought Pro before? Tap Restore Purchases in Everdot's Settings.

Face ID lock

If Face ID doesn't work, use your iPhone passcode on the lock screen. You can turn the lock off in Everdot's Settings.

Your data

Everdot doesn't collect any data. Export everything as a spreadsheet with Settings → Export as CSV. Read the privacy policy.

Medical disclaimer

Everdot records what you enter. It doesn't give medical advice, check doses or warn about interactions. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.
